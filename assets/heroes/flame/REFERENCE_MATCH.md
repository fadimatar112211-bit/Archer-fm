# Flame Knight — reference match contract

The uploaded four-hero reference sheet is the visual source of truth for Flame Knight. This contract applies only to Flame and must not alter Ranger, Shadow, or Storm.

## Silhouette and proportions
- Chibi/anime fantasy proportions: oversized head, compact torso, short sturdy legs, expressive face.
- Bright, swept-up flame-red/orange spiky hair with multiple layered locks; no brown-dominant hair.
- Fully visible face, strong dark eyebrows, vivid crimson/red eyes, small nose and determined expression.
- No helmet or hood. Red scarf wrapped around the neck, with a visible flowing tail.

## Armour and clothing
- Red and deep crimson armour over a near-black under-suit.
- Large gold-trimmed shoulder pauldron, especially prominent on the character's right shoulder in the reference.
- Gold-edged chest and waist armour, broad belt with a central gold buckle, gold forearm guards and knee/shin plates.
- Dark brown leather gloves/boots and dark trousers.
- Flowing deep-red cape behind the body, with a visible flame-cut lower silhouette.

## Weapon and VFX
- Oversized broad greatsword, held in one hand and angled diagonally down across the character's side.
- Bright orange/red flaming blade with a readable gold crossguard and hilt; blade must remain distinguishable from hand VFX.
- Controlled orange ember/flame accents around the sword and character; they must not obscure the face, armour, or sword silhouette.

## Color intent
- Hair: deep red roots/shadows, saturated scarlet midtones, vivid orange-red highlights.
- Armour: scarlet/crimson, blackened panels, warm gold trim.
- Eyes: crimson red.
- Fire: orange-red outer glow with a warm yellow core.
- Avoid muddy brown hair, orange-only armour, or a yellow/gold face/skin cast.

## Acceptance review
A reviewer must inspect front and three-quarter idle renders plus attack and skill frames at full resolution. Confirm the face, hair silhouette, scarf, shoulder armour, cape, greatsword silhouette, and palette against the supplied reference. A successful Blender export or atlas count alone does not prove visual fidelity. Do not mark Flame complete until this visual review passes and all eight actions, eight directions, GLB skinning, Flutter analysis/tests, and Android debug build pass.

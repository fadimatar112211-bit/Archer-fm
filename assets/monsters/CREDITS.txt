Changes made to the original models (required by CC BY 4.0 to be stated):
- Proportions changed to a chibi look by scaling bones (larger head, shorter legs/arms) and a uniform root scale.
- Polygon count reduced automatically (edge-collapse decimation).
- Animation "Hit" added; "Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Roar, Die" kept from the originals with ground-contact corrections (Root translation).
- Textures were not repainted; they are the original images, extracted unchanged into textures/ folders.

Each GLB also carries this credit in its asset.copyright field.
